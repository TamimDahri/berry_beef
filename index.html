how the week works

One delivery day, so nothing sits in a warehouse. We only order from the farm what you have ordered from us.

  1. By Monday, 10pmSend us your order on WhatsApp.
  2. TuesdayWe send your order to the farm in Cameron Highlands.
  3. WednesdayThe farm packs it and a bus brings it down to Terminal Bersepadu Selatan.
  4. Wednesday, 3pm to 9pmOur riders pick it up at the terminal and bring it to your door.

this is what lands at your door.

A 2kg order arrives in the farm's own box, the way it left Cameron Highlands. Nothing is repacked or sorted again in KL.

Not home? Tell us where to leave it: with the guardhouse, in the parcel room or at the lobby. Ask and your rider will send a photo once it is there.

questions

How do I order?

Tap Order on WhatsApp. A message opens with the details we need: your name, address and unit, and how many kilos of each kind. Send it before Monday, 10pm and we confirm by WhatsApp.

How do I pay?

By QR or cash when your strawberries arrive. The rider tells you the amount.

Can I order the same thing every week?

Yes. Tell us your usual order once and we keep your address and unit on file, so next time you only need to say "same as last week".

Why only on Wednesdays?

Strawberries are best within a day or two of picking. One delivery day means we order exactly what was ordered from us, and you get it the day it comes down from the farm.

Something was wrong with my strawberries.

Send us a photo on WhatsApp the same day so we can sort it out.
